# Known issues and limits

This is an experimental legacy CS:GO VR modification. Reports from testers do not all identify the exact F6 version and should **not** be presented as reproduced F6 defects.

- SSG optics and nearby AUG scope pixelation have been reported. Current F6 optics claim only AUG/AWP.
- Audio turning with joystick instead of head direction reported.
- Grenade throw direction sometimes not aligned with intended hand aim reported.
- Occasional disappearing enemies reported, possibly after death/bot takeover; cause unknown.
- Author Meta PCVR test positive. Other OpenXR runtime/headset compatibility not established. Prior PSVR2/WMR/SteamVR and VDXR reports are not pinned to F6.
- New game frames/s, GPU source issue rate and xrEndFrame cadence are different metrics.
- Left game arm is not an independent 6DoF tracked VR hand.
- Local/offline only. No secure matchmaking or online support.

When filing an issue, attach a local `REPORTS` ZIP after reviewing it, plus game/build, weapon, headset, runtime, map and repro steps. The mod does not upload reports automatically.
